    Questions

    What's the cut-off for next-day delivery?+

    Approve the artwork by 2 PM and we deliver next day by 11 AM in Delhi NCR. After 2 PM, delivery moves one day later.

    How many kits can you do in 24 hours?+

    It depends on the print method. UV and laser engraving are fastest; screen printing and offset need drying time. Tell us the headcount and we'll confirm before you approve.

    How do I get pricing?+

    WhatsApp or call us on +91 85888 77032 with the occasion, headcount and date. We check stock and share the price for your kit, with product, printing and delivery listed separately.

    Do I pay before or after?+

    After you approve the quote and the mock-up we share a payment link. Every order comes with a GST invoice.

    Do you work with schools, universities and government offices?+

    Yes. We accept purchase orders, raise GST invoices in the institution's name and deliver to the department, school or venue.

    Can you print invites and event material too?+

    Yes. Invites, name cards, badges and printed boxes run on our offset and UV presses, on the same next-day timeline.
